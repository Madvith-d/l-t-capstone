SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — NOT AN OFFICIAL COLLEGE POLICY

Attendance:
For the academic-agent demonstration dataset only, students must maintain at least 85 percent attendance in each course to be eligible for the end-semester examination. A student below 85 percent attendance must meet the academic adviser and may be declared ineligible for that examination unless an approved exception applies.

Examination Guidelines:
For this synthetic demonstration, students must bring their identity card and arrive thirty minutes before the examination. Prohibited electronic devices must remain outside the examination room.

Internal Assessment:
The demonstration internal assessment consists of two tests worth 20 marks each and one assignment worth 10 marks. This rule exists only to exercise retrieval and must not be treated as a real institution's policy.
